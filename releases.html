Release notes

What changed in each version of FindMe. The newest is at the top.

Version 1.0.1 26 November 2026Latest

  • New: Check for updates, More apps and FindMe website in Settings, About. Each opens a page in your browser; FindMe itself still has no internet permission.
  • The bottom bar now sits flat along the bottom of the screen, with each icon above its label and a pill behind the page you are on.
  • A proper Android app icon, so the pin fills its shape on the home screen instead of sitting small inside a white square, plus a themed icon for Android 13 and later.
  • Choosing Custom distance from the movement-distance list now opens the distance box, and a saved custom distance shows its metres.
  • Settings shows the theme, accent colour and session length in plain words, and the accent list ticks the colour you are using.
  • A new one-hour reminder now shows 60 minutes remaining, not 59.
  • More info now reads Developed by Jonathan Jackson.

Version 1.0.0 24 November 2026

  • First release. A GPS fix turned into a message with Google Maps and Waze links, coordinates, the time, your name and an optional note.
  • Send by WhatsApp, text message, email or any other app, after checking the message.
  • Timed check-in or movement reminders that ask Have you changed location?, with Share update, Still here and Stop.
  • No internet permission, encrypted storage, backups and screenshots blocked, optional app lock, home-screen shortcut and Quick Settings tile.

Checking your download

The file for version 1.0.1 is called FindMe.apk. It is 455 KB (465,765 bytes). Its SHA-256 checksum is:

f1a3c18a19f8cc6b40731b056b990ef749b813a4780a97957462ccd5bb3643d7

On Windows, run certutil -hashfile FindMe.apk SHA256 and compare. Every version is signed with the same key, so each one installs over the last and keeps what you have saved.

Older versions are on the GitHub releases page.
