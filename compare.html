What FindMe does not do

FindMe is not on Google Play, so you install it from this site. It does not show where other people are, keep a history, send alerts when someone arrives, or call for help. It cannot tell whether your message was read.

Where the others do more

Life360 shows a whole family on one live map, with place alerts, location history and, with membership, crash detection and SOS. Glympse shares a live, moving location for a set time that the other person watches in a browser. Both are on Google Play.

Why one point

A single point you choose to send is the most private way to say where you are: nobody, including the developer, can follow you afterwards.
