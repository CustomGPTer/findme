FindMe 1.0.1: Check for updates, More apps and a new bottom bar.

- Settings, About now has Check for updates, More apps and FindMe website. Each opens a page in your browser; FindMe itself still has no internet permission
- The bottom bar sits flat along the bottom of the screen, with each icon above its label and a pill behind the page you are on
- A proper Android app icon, so the pin fills its shape on the home screen, plus a themed icon on Android 13 and later
- Choosing Custom distance from the movement-distance list now opens the distance box, and a saved custom distance shows its metres
- Settings shows the theme, accent colour and session length in plain words, and the accent list ticks the colour you are using
- A new one-hour reminder shows 60 minutes remaining, not 59

Install it over 1.0.0: your name and settings are kept. Needs Android 10 or later.
