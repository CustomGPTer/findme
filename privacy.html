Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026. This policy covers the FindMe app for Android (package com.jonjackson.findme) and this website. FindMe is made by Jonathan Jackson, an individual developer in the United Kingdom.

The short version

  • FindMe has no internet permission. It cannot send anything to the developer or anyone else.
  • Your location leaves your phone only in a message you send yourself, through the app you choose.
  • There is no account, no adverts, no analytics and no location history.
  • What FindMe keeps on the phone is encrypted, and left out of backups.

Location

FindMe asks for precise location so it can get a GPS fix when you tap share. It uses Android's GPS directly, not a network location service. Movement reminders read GPS only while a reminder you started is running, with a notification on show and a Stop button. FindMe never asks for background location.

When you share

The message holds your coordinates, the time, the GPS accuracy, and any name and note you add. WhatsApp, Messages, email and other apps send it under their own privacy policies. When the other person opens a Google Maps or Waze link, that service receives the location. The links carry no FindMe tracking code and no link shortener.

What stays on your phone

Your display name, settings, a message waiting to be sent and an active reminder are encrypted with AES-GCM and a key held in Android Keystore. Android's cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are switched off for FindMe. A waiting message expires after an hour, and a reminder's location is deleted when it ends or you stop it. Restarting the phone clears both.

What the app can reach

Precise location, notifications (for reminders), a location foreground service (only for movement reminders you start), fingerprint or screen lock (only for the optional app lock), vibration, and starting after a restart (to clear reminders, never to start tracking). No internet, contacts, SMS, camera, microphone or storage access.

Links in the app

Check for updates, More apps and the FindMe website link in Settings open pages on this site in your web browser. Your browser makes that connection, not FindMe.

Deleting your data

Settings, Delete active and prepared locations stops reminders and removes stored coordinates. Uninstalling FindMe removes everything it kept. Neither can recall a message you have already sent.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies and no analytics. GitHub may keep standard web server logs, such as IP addresses, for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

FindMe collects nothing from anyone. A child sharing their location should do so with a parent or carer's agreement.

Contact
